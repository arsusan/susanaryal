Papers & Continued Learning

Research papers
Attention Is All You Need — Vaswani et al., 2017
The paper that introduced the Transformer architecture — the backbone behind almost every modern LLM, including the agent systems I build with LangGraph.
BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers — Devlin et al., 2018
How masked-language-model pretraining produces representations that transfer across NLP tasks.
Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (GPT-3) — Brown et al., 2020
Scaling laws and in-context / few-shot learning — the ideas underpinning most of today's prompting and agent-evaluation work.
Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition (ResNet) — He et al., 2015
Residual connections and why very deep CNNs became trainable — background reading behind the MRI tumor-detection project above.
